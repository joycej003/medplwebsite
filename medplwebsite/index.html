My Creative Statement

Joyce is a new media artist interested in experimenting with different techniques and forms of visual expression to explore the possibilities of digital media. She enjoys exploring a wide range of visual styles and aesthetics and hopes to explore them through digital art, illustration, and visual design. Color, composition, and atmosphere are important aspects of the visuals she appreciates. She is particularly interested in how these elements can transform an image and create particular feelings. As she continues developing her creative practice, she hopes to discover her own style and find new ways to create imaginative and visually engaging work.
